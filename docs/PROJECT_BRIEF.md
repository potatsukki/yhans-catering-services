# Project Brief

## Project

Build a production-quality, mobile-friendly, three-page marketing website for **Yhan's Catering Services**.

## Primary goal

Create a professional web presence that helps prospective customers:

- understand what Yhan's Catering Services offers;
- view confirmed packages and pricing;
- learn which events and locations are served;
- contact the business directly through Facebook, email, phone, or Viber;
- trust the business through its history, registration, capabilities, and professional presentation.

## Scope

The website has exactly three public routes:

1. `/` — Home
2. `/packages` — Packages & Services
3. `/about-contact` — About & Contact

A friendly not-found route may be added.

## Product type

This is a static marketing website and landing-page system.

It does **not** require:

- a backend;
- authentication;
- an admin dashboard;
- a database;
- online payment processing;
- a booking engine;
- customer accounts;
- a website inquiry form;
- a CMS.

All important calls to action should send visitors to:

- Facebook;
- email;
- phone;
- Viber-compatible phone numbers.

## Visual direction

Use the attached generated mockups as the primary design target:

- warm cream and light beige backgrounds;
- deep burgundy primary color;
- restrained gold accents;
- elegant serif display typography;
- clean sans-serif body typography;
- a script font used only for short accent phrases;
- food and event photography;
- rounded cards;
- subtle borders and shadows;
- decorative lines and flourishes;
- premium but welcoming Filipino catering identity.

The design must look custom-built. Do not use a generic SaaS template or dashboard style.

## Audience

- families planning weddings, debuts, baptisms, graduations, and gatherings;
- event organizers;
- schools and seminar organizers;
- offices and corporate clients;
- call centers ordering packed meals;
- customers ordering food trays;
- clients seeking catering plus partner-arranged event add-ons.

## Core conversions

Primary:

- Message on Facebook
- Request a quote through Facebook
- Call or text the business
- Send an email

Secondary:

- View packages
- Read the business story
- Check service areas
- Review booking steps

## Prototype content policy

Real customer photos and testimonials will be added later.

During development:

- use tasteful placeholder catering and event images;
- do not invent real customer names;
- do not fabricate endorsements;
- use neutral placeholder review blocks or omit testimonials;
- document every placeholder asset in `docs/PROGRESS.md`;
- make replacing placeholders straightforward through centralized data and asset files.

## Quality target

The completed website must be:

- visually close to the attached mockups;
- responsive from small mobile screens to large desktop displays;
- keyboard accessible;
- screen-reader friendly;
- fast and image-conscious;
- SEO-ready;
- covered by unit and end-to-end tests;
- deployable to Vercel as a client-side React application.
